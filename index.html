Outbound, engineered

Cold email that lands in the inbox, not the spam folder.

Most outbound fails before anyone reads a word. We build the sending layer properly, then write copy worth opening.

Separate sending domains

Campaigns run on look-alike domains, so your main domain and team inboxes stay protected.

Authenticated and warmed

Every mailbox gets SPF, DKIM and DMARC, plus a warm-up period before the first real email goes out.

Low volume per mailbox

Volume is spread across many mailboxes at a safe daily cap, which keeps reputation healthy as you scale.

Signal-led copy

Each email references a real reason to reach out today: a hire, a funding round, a new tool, a new market.

FAQ

Questions we get asked.

Do you only work with B2B companies?

Mostly, yes. Our outbound and automation work is built for B2B sales cycles. Brand, web and content work suits B2C brands too.

Will cold email hurt my company's domain?

No. We send from separate domains set up just for outreach, so your main domain and day-to-day email are never at risk.

Can we start with just one service?

Yes. Many clients start with a single sprint, like a deliverability fix or a website, and add more once they see results.

Do you use our tools or yours?

Either. We happily work inside your existing CRM and accounts, or set up and own the stack for you.

How quickly will we see results?

Outbound campaigns usually go live within three to four weeks, after domains are warmed. SEO and brand work take longer to compound.
